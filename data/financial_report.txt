PERSONAL EXPENSE LOG - 2024

January 2024
Man, January was rough. Started the year feeling optimistic but money just flew out the door. My rent is $1,200 and I paid that on the 1st like always. Groceries for the month probably came to around $350 - I tried to meal prep but ended up eating out more than I wanted to. My car insurance hit me with $145 for the month, which is fine I guess. What really got me was my phone bill came in at $85 and then I had to drop $200 on new winter tires because the old ones were getting bald and the weather was sketchy. I didn't expect that expense at all. By the end of January I had maybe saved $150 which is pathetic. I really need to get my spending under control.

February 2024
February was actually better than expected. Paid rent again - $1,200. Did pretty good on groceries this time, spent about $280 because I actually stuck to a shopping list. The utility bill was higher than normal though, came in at $95 for heating costs. Car insurance again at $145. I treated myself to a couple of dinners out with friends, probably spent $120 on that. Got my haircut for $50 which I needed. The good news is my paycheck was the same but I was more careful so I managed to save about $400 this month. That felt good.

March 2024
March sucked. Everything was fine until my computer died. Literally just stopped working. I had to spend $800 to get it repaired because apparently it was a motherboard issue or something. That absolutely destroyed my budget for the month. Rent $1,200, groceries $310, phone $85, car insurance $145, utilities $65. The computer repair was the killer though. I also had to get gas which was like $50 for the month. After all that I didn't save anything. Actually I think I dipped into my emergency fund a bit which was not ideal. This is why I need that fund I guess but it's so frustrating when stuff like this happens out of nowhere.

April 2024
April was recovery month. Tried to get back on track after the computer disaster. Rent $1,200, groceries about $290, utilities $55 because it was getting warmer. Phone $85. Got my car's oil changed which was $45. I went out to eat more than I should have - probably spent $130 on restaurants and coffee shops. Bought some new clothes because my work pants were falling apart, spent about $75. By the end of the month I scraped together about $200 in savings but still recovering from March. My paycheck is pretty consistent around $2,500 before taxes, so after taxes I get like $1,850 monthly. It's tight with rent being so high but I'm managing.

May 2024
May was decent actually. The weather got nice so I wasn't spending as much on utilities - just $40 this month. Rent $1,200 of course, groceries $320, phone $85. Car insurance $145. I had a birthday so I went out with friends and spent about $95 on drinks and food. Bought a birthday gift for someone else which was $35. What surprised me was my water bill came in higher than expected at $30 - I think I was watering plants or something. By May I was feeling a little better and managed to save $225. Still not amazing but better than the March disaster.

June 2024
June was actually good for savings! Rent $1,200, groceries $275 because I was on a roll with meal prepping. Utilities $45. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas for the car was about $55. I only went out to eat like twice which was maybe $65 total. Bought some work stuff and snacks which was probably $40. Managed to save $500 this month! That felt really good. I think I'm finally getting into a rhythm with my spending. I need to keep this up because I'm trying to build my emergency fund back up after the computer situation.

July 2024
July was vacation month which killed my budget but also recharged my soul. Rent $1,200. I took a week off and drove to the beach with some friends. Gas was $120 for the trip, and I paid for like half the hotel which came to $200 for my share. Food and stuff while traveling was probably $180. My normal groceries for the month were maybe $200 since I was gone half the month. Utilities $50. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. So all in I probably only saved like $100 but honestly it was worth it. Sometimes you need to take a break and not stress about every dollar.

August 2024
August I tried to recover again. Rent $1,200. Groceries $340 because I was stocking up. Utilities $60 with some AC use. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas about $60. I had to buy some stuff for work - like a new bag and shoes - spent about $110. Bought some random stuff I didn't need probably $50. Went out to eat maybe $85. By August I realized I was being way too relaxed with my spending. Saved maybe $150. I really need to be more disciplined because the year is halfway through and my savings aren't where they should be.

September 2024
September was when reality hit me. Rent $1,200. Then my AC unit started making a weird noise and I took it to the shop. It was the compressor - $650 to fix. That was NOT in my budget. So rent, then that huge repair. Groceries were $300. Utilities $55. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas $50. Went out to eat like $60. I had a couple unexpected friend hangs where we ordered food, maybe $40. I couldn't save anything in September. The AC repair basically wiped me out. I'm really glad I have an emergency fund because I don't know what I'd do without it. People say you need 3-6 months of expenses saved and I'm starting to see why.

October 2024
October I'm trying to get back on track after September. Rent $1,200. Got my car inspected which was $35. Groceries about $310. Utilities $50. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas was $65. I splurged on a couple nice dinners out maybe $100 total. Bought myself a coffee maker I've been wanting for $45 - I know I shouldn't have but I needed it for my mental health I guess. Only managed to save like $80. I keep saying I'm going to get disciplined but then something comes up or I just want to treat myself. By October I'm realizing my rent is way too high for my income. I need to either find a cheaper place or get a better paying job.

November 2024
November was rough emotionally. Rent $1,200. Groceries $290. Utilities $65 because heating season is starting. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas $70. I had to buy a winter jacket which was $95. I didn't go out much because I was feeling kinda down, so eating out was only like $40. Bought some random stuff probably $50. But then my cat got sick and vet bills were $400. That was stressful. Had to charge it to my credit card because I didn't have the cash. So in November I saved literally $0 and went into debt because of the vet bill. The credit card is now hanging over my head. I'm stressed.

December 2024
December I'm in recovery mode again. Rent $1,200. Had to pay down some of the credit card debt from the vet visit, so I transferred $250. Groceries $320. Utilities $70 for heating. Phone $85. Car insurance $145. Gas $55. Christmas shopping was tight - I spent about $180 on gifts which felt cheap but it's what I could afford. Went out to eat a couple times for $75. Bought some end of year stuff probably $40. Tried to save something and managed $130 but honestly I'm just trying to finish the year without going backwards more. Looking back at 2024, I probably saved like $2,000 total which sounds okay but with all the unexpected expenses (computer, AC, vet bills) I feel like I barely made progress. I need to think about what to do differently in 2025. Maybe I need a side gig or need to find a cheaper place. My rent is killing me. Anyway, that's it for 2024. Onto 2025 hoping for less disasters.

END OF YEAR REFLECTION

So looking back at the whole year, I had to pay rent every single month - that's $14,400 right there. Groceries were consistently $280-320, so probably around $3,500 for the year. Car insurance every month $145, so $1,740. Phone $85 a month, so $1,020. Gas varied but probably $700 total. Utilities were seasonal, maybe $750 total. All those basics add up to like $22,000+ already and that's without food out, entertainment, or anything else. When unexpected stuff hits like the computer and AC and vet bills, it just derails everything. I feel like I'm on a hamster wheel - get paid, pay bills, have some random expense, repeat. My salary is like $30,000 a year before taxes so after taxes it's maybe $24,000. That's barely more than my rent plus basic stuff. I really need a change in 2025. Either I need to increase my income or decrease my expenses. Or both. This is exhausting.
